# Technology selection

Read when choosing a stack, vendor, or platform.

Evaluate options against the developer's existing knowledge, build speed, ecosystem and maintenance, performance, deployment, operational burden, cost, lock-in, portability, and migration difficulty. Keep the comparison small and recommend the best fit, not the most popular option.

Check the relevant layer only: frontend, backend, database, authentication, hosting, infrastructure, or AI framework. For managed services, separate prototype, launch, and growth costs and verify official pricing or limits when they can change the decision. Include operational responsibilities separately from subscription claims.

For each material choice, state what is gained, given up, harder later, and the condition that would make the choice wrong.
