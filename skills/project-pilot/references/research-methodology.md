# Research methodology

Read when external research or comparable projects could change a recommendation.

## Source order

Prefer official documentation, official repositories, standards, specifications, and maintainer-authored material. Use reputable secondary sources only when primary sources do not answer the question.

## Comparable projects

Choose one direct comparable when available, then add an adjacent project, official template, or contrasting lighter/heavier option only when it changes the decision. For each, record relevance, observed stack or architecture, transferable patterns, non-transferable details, maintenance signal, and limits. Never rank by stars alone or invent activity statistics.

## Evidence ledger

Track the claim, source, exact observed date for time-sensitive facts, what it supports, and what it does not establish. Keep private identifiers and unpublished context out of public queries.

## Stop rule

Stop when material recommendations are supported, the main alternative is understood, and remaining uncertainty is disclosed. After the initial evidence set, allow at most one targeted follow-up for unresolved claims; two consecutive no-progress lookups end the pass. Do not repeat searches to manufacture certainty.
